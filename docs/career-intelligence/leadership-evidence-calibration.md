# Leadership and specialist requirements calibration

This correction recognizes German and English delivery responsibilities separately
from specialist qualifications. Roadmaps, project leadership, operational handover,
stakeholder coordination and procurement transformation can establish a selective
transformation bridge. Generic milestones, dependencies and partner mentions alone
cannot establish that bridge. Engineering-title exclusions remain prior gates.

`specialist_requirements.py` matches explicit qualification phrases and reports
source matches, the relevant configured capability, actual candidate evidence, and
supported/partial/not-documented status. Limited coding/prototype work does not
satisfy professional architecture, finance consulting, procurement or Power BI
modelling requirements. These gaps use the existing core-gap cap and WATCH/SKIP
policy; no employer-specific rules or evidence additions are introduced.

A captured non-Berlin job location plus an explicit mobile-work percentage below
100 is not treated as fully remote. The current rule covers named outside-region
cities, preserves Berlin options, and records the exact location and percentage
text. Unknown travel remains unknown. Location does not contribute to candidacy.

The new exact versioned fixture `leadership_requirements_v1.json` retains the three
captured role descriptions for regression review. Existing calibration fixtures and
the reviewed 30-record legacy recovery manifest remain unchanged. Reassessment uses
the previous combined preview's exact 43 selected identities; both MOIA records
remain unselected and unchanged in the complete 45-record candidate generation.

The preliminary `leadership-review-2026-09-23` preview is superseded by
`leadership-review-final-2026-09-23`. The final preview removes a generic-dependency
bridge match that increased an unrelated role's score. Use only the final preview
for review. No apply has been run.
